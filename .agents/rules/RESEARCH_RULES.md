# Research Rules

- Use the canonical NSE-resolved symbol and identify the research run/as-of context.
- Prefer the compact evidence pack, readiness, reconciliation, source health, and evidence quality artifacts before raw files.
- Never compare financial metrics across incompatible periods, units, accounting bases, or standalone/consolidated views without labeling the mismatch.
- Distinguish current market price from delayed/last-close/period-end price.
- Treat news sentiment as a heuristic, not a fact about future returns.
- Corporate events remain confirmed only when a trusted source supports that status.
- Label unsupported fields as unavailable; do not backfill from memory.
- Any new metric must specify formula, units, inputs, time basis, and tests.
- Every conclusion should have supporting evidence and the strongest counter-evidence.
- Explain limitations and what additional evidence would change the conclusion.
