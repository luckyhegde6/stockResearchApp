# v1.45 — NSE NextApi Stability & Datewise Evidence

## Fixes
- Fixed `quoteToFacts` import in `src/adapters/nse.ts`. The v1.44 runtime could fail with `quoteToFacts is not defined` even though the normalizer exported it.
- Fixed asynchronous catalyst normalization in `src/lib/individual-stock-evidence.ts`: `normalizeNextAnnouncements()` is awaited before iteration.
- These two runtime defects blocked/partially corrupted the deterministic preparation phase after successful acquisition.

## NSE architecture
The NSE NextApi layer remains deterministic and reusable. Endpoint definitions belong in `src/lib/nse-nextapi-registry.ts`; request execution belongs in the NSE adapter/client; normalization belongs in `src/lib/nse-nextapi-normalizer.ts` and `src/lib/nse-nextapi-normalizers.ts`.

## Datewise research
Individual-stock research remains short-lived under `research/<SYMBOL>/`. v1.44+ NextApi artifacts are grouped by function under `raw/nse-api/nextapi/`. A datewise snapshot/index should point to the acquired data without duplicating raw payloads.
