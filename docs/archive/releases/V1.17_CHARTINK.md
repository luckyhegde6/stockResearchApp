# v1.17 — Chartink Strategy & Screening Evidence

Adds deterministic Chartink strategy acquisition to the stock-research pipeline. The collector captures configured public strategy pages, the `/screener/process` results used by the UI when available, CSV exports when the visible CSV action works, strategy metadata/scan clauses, and category-level stock baskets. It also records whether the target ticker matched each configured Long, Short, Intraday or Swing strategy.

No LLM is used.
