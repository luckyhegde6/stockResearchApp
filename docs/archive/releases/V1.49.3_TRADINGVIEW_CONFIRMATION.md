# v1.49.3 — TradingView Metrics Surface Confirmation

## Problem
The Metrics launcher was found and clicked, and surface clicks were reported as successful, but all five UI artifacts remained `partial` because confirmation was too permissive about where the surface label came from and too strict about the post-click SPA state.

## Fix
- Scope surface-item selection to a visible Metrics popover/container containing multiple sibling surface labels.
- Click the exact requested surface by coordinates inside that popover.
- Poll the post-click SPA state for up to `TRADINGVIEW_UI_CONFIRM_TIMEOUT_MS` (default 9s).
- Confirm with a surface-specific heading/content signature or a route transition.
- Do not treat the generic right-sidebar News card as the confirmation for the News surface.
- Keep screenshots even when confirmation fails.
- Emit the selected container/item geometry in `ui-<surface>.json` for debugging.
