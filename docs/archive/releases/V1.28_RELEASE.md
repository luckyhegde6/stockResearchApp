# v1.28 — Dynamic Instrument Routing + Deterministic Analysis Preparation

## TradingView
- Dynamically builds `https://in.tradingview.com/chart/?symbol=EXCHANGE%3ASYMBOL` from the requested ticker.
- Captures 1D, 5Y and All Supercharts screenshots under the ticker research directory.
- Persists chart URL, final URL, range selection result and chart-type selection metadata.

## NSE series
- Adds a machine-readable series legend based on the supplied NSE series table.
- Preserves raw series codes and classifies equity/SME/special instruments before universe building.

## MarkItDown
- Deterministically converts supported raw documents into Markdown.
- Preserves original files and provenance.
- Adds a visual-evidence index for screenshots rather than pretending screenshots were text-converted.

## Analysis readiness
- Generates `analysis-readiness.json`.
- `npm run analyze -- TICKER` refuses to call the LLM unless the deterministic readiness gate passes.
- The evidence bundle includes readiness state.
