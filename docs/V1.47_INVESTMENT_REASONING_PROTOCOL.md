# V1.47 Investment Reasoning Protocol

## Purpose

Turn the deterministic evidence package into an institutional-style investment decision. No acquisition occurs here.

## Evidence order

`analysis-evidence-pack.json` → `analysis-inputs.json` → `evidence-contract.json` → reconciliation → MarkItDown documents → visual evidence.

## Decision stack

1. Identity/data audit
2. Business quality
3. Financial quality
4. Management
5. Valuation
6. Technicals
7. Ownership
8. Catalysts
9. Risks
10. Peers/alternatives
11. Contrarian test
12. Scenarios
13. Recommendation

## Evidence citation

Material narrative strings should contain references such as `[nse-symbol-data|market_cap]`, `[nse-financial-status|Jun-2026]`, or another identifier actually present in the supplied evidence package.

## Confidence

Confidence measures evidence reliability. Conviction measures attractiveness. Missing evidence lowers confidence, not truth.

## Optional sources

Screener/Tijori conference-call fetch failures are advisory when the six core stock evidence domains are complete. Chartink is optional for individual stock research. BSE is excluded.

## No false precision

Use ranges and explicit assumptions instead of unsupported numerical targets.
